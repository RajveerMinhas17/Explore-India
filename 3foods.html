Famous Indian Foods

Indian food is known all over the world for its rich spices and flavours. Every region of India has its own special dishes, from spicy curries to sweet desserts. Food is a big part of Indian culture and every festival has its own special food.

Top 5 Indian Dishes

  1. Biryani
  2. Butter Chicken
  3. Dosa
  4. Chole Bhature
  5. Gulab Jamun

Food Categories

  • North Indian
  • South Indian
  • Street Food
  • Desserts

Dish Descriptions

Butter Chicken
A creamy tomato-based curry with tender pieces of chicken.
Biryani
A spiced rice dish cooked with meat or vegetables.
Dosa
A thin crispy South Indian pancake made from rice and lentil batter.
Gulab Jamun
A sweet dessert made of milk balls soaked in sugar syrup.
